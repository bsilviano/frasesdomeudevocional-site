# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Frases do Meu Devocional is a personal project that publishes short faith-based videos (a phrase over a background image with the creator's own music) on social media: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.

## What data is collected

This website does not collect, store or share personal data from visitors.

## Use of access to the YouTube account

The app uses the authorization of the channel owner only to upload the project's videos to the project's own channel. No data from other people is read, stored or shared.

## Use of access to the Instagram and TikTok accounts

In the same way, the app uses the authorization of the owner of the Instagram and TikTok accounts only to publish the project's own videos to those accounts. With TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload) the app stores only the OAuth tokens needed to post, in a private repository. No data from other TikTok users is read, stored, sold or shared, and no data is shared with third parties.

## Use of access to the Pinterest account

The app uses the authorization of the Pinterest account owner, through the Pinterest API, only to publish the project's own videos as Pins on a board of the owner's own account. The permissions used are limited to reading and creating Pins and boards of that same account. The app stores only the access tokens needed to publish, in a private repository. No data from other Pinterest users is read, stored, sold or shared, and no data is shared with third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time in the Pinterest account settings.
