# How It Works

1. The creator selects and reviews each phrase.
2. The phrase is combined with a background image and the creator's own music in a short vertical video.
3. The creator approves each video.
4. The project's app posts it to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest accounts, using the creator's authorization (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API). On Pinterest, videos become Pins on the "Frases" board of @frasesdomeudevocional, up to 4 times a day.

The app does not read, store or share data from other users. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-en.html).
